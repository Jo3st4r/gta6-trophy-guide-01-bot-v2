      Sources & honesty

      The trophy names, descriptions and grades in this guide are the list reported in agreement by multiple outlets on 18 November 2026. Rockstar and PlayStation had not published an official trophy list when this guide was built (20 November 2026), and the PSNProfiles community has called the circulating named list “probably fake” — only the count of 55 traces to a dataminer claim. Treat every name, grade and guide step here as reported, not confirmed: verify against your console’s trophy list at launch.

        Platinum Road is an unofficial fan guide. It uses no Rockstar Games logo, official artwork, box art or screenshot of the game. All guide text is written for this entry; sources are cited, never copied.
